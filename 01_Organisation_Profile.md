# 01. Organisation Profile & Statutory Constitution
## British Broadcasting Corporation (BBC)

- **Sector:** UK Public Media & Broadcasting
- **Founded:** 18 October 1922 (Royal Charter established 1927)
- **Headquarters:** Broadcasting House, Portland Place, London W1A 1AA
- **Regulator:** Ofcom (Office of Communications) under the BBC Royal Charter
- **Statutory Basis & Governance:** BBC Board, led by Chair and Director-General Tim Davie, regulated by Ofcom
- **Tagline:** "Nation Shall Speak Peace Unto Nation — Public Service Broadcasting & Digital Media"

### Executive Mission Overview
The BBC is the world’s leading public service broadcaster, providing impartial news, television, radio, BBC iPlayer, BBC Sounds, and educational content to millions of daily users across the UK and internationally. Its web and mobile platforms handle hundreds of millions of daily requests with strict uptime, accessibility, and editorial accuracy mandates.
